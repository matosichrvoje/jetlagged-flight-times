Jetlagged: Privacy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Jetlagged has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your trips, sleep times and plans are stored on your iPhone.

What leaves your phone

  • Flight numbers you type. When you enter a flight number, only that number is sent to adsbdb.com to find its airports.
  • Airline time tables. The app downloads a public file of typical flight times for that airline (for example UAE.json) from GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address like any website.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn how the app is used, Jetlagged sends two kinds of anonymous events to our own server (a Cloudflare Worker we run; no third-party analytics company is involved):

  • App use, such as opening the app, viewing a plan, or turning reminders on. These carry a random ID created on your phone that is replaced every month, so we can count how many people use the app without following anyone over time.
  • Trips you save: airline, flight number, route (airport codes), departure date, number of flights, and the time-zone shift. These carry no ID at all, so a trip cannot be linked to your phone, to you, or to your other trips.

Each event also includes the app and iOS version, and the country the request came from (worked out by Cloudflare). We never store your IP address, name, email, contacts, precise location, or any advertising identifier, and we never sell or share this data.

You can switch this off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage. Your sleep times and the times of your flights are never sent.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders and wake-up alarms are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. They never go through a server.

Deleting your data

Delete a trip in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your phone. Anonymous statistics can't be traced back to you, so there's nothing on our side that's linked to you to delete.
