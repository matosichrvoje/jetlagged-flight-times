Jetlagged: Privacy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Jetlagged has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your trips, sleep times and plans are stored only on your iPhone.

What leaves your phone

  • Flight numbers you type. When you enter a flight number, only that number is sent to adsbdb.com to find its airports.
  • Airline time tables. The app downloads a public file of typical flight times for that airline (for example UAE.json) from GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address like any website.

Nothing else is sent: not your trips, dates, sleep times or anything that identifies you.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders and wake-up alarms are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. They never go through a server.

Deleting your data

Delete a trip in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
